# MOT20 M23-8—M23-12 protocol audit (2026-07-18)

## Scope

This note records the continuation of the M23 deployable-association audit on
MOT20-train sequences 01/02/03/05. The parent tracker is:

```text
outputs/assocriskbench_p15_20260714/
  fused_a42_adaptive_risk_eval/l1_r2_q75_adaptive_priority/track_results
```

Parent TrackEval: HOTA 78.763497, DetA 81.551695, AssA 76.123375,
IDSW 901.

The protocol labels used below are deliberately strict:

- **GT oracle / nondeployable**: GT is used to choose an action or identity.
- **GT-free exploratory**: inference does not read GT and held-sequence model
  fitting is excluded, but the policy has not passed nested policy selection.
- **Deployable candidate**: reserved for a fully fixed or nested
  sequence-LOSO policy. None of the new M23-8—M23-11 results has this label.

## Micrograph and utility-model evidence

The chunk-30 graph contains 38,818 microtracklets and 615,042 candidate edges:

| Sequence | Microtracklets | Candidate edges |
|---|---:|---:|
| MOT20-01 | 698 | 10,295 |
| MOT20-02 | 5,290 | 84,343 |
| MOT20-03 | 10,602 | 166,844 |
| MOT20-05 | 22,228 | 353,560 |

The first same-ID LOSO model was dominated by source-adjacent positives. On
cross-track edges its held-sequence AP was 0.3323 / 0.1634 / 0.0848 / 0.1676,
which was insufficient as a policy target.

M23-11 therefore uses a signed AssA-aligned edge utility label:

```text
delta = AssA contribution after merging two chunks
      - AssA contribution when the chunks remain separate
```

The GT-derived utility and diagnostics are training/evaluation columns only.
Inference uses an explicit GT-free feature allowlist. Separate source and
cross HistGradientBoosting classifier/regressor pairs are fit with strict
outer sequence-LOSO.

For held-out cross edges, the top predicted utility ranks have positive net
true proxy utility on every sequence. Examples:

| Held sequence | top-10 net utility | top-100 net utility | top-1000 net utility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MOT20-01 | +26.320 | +142.602 | -16.520 |
| MOT20-02 | +21.557 | +40.442 | +236.017 |
| MOT20-03 | +17.264 | +87.936 | +108.389 |
| MOT20-05 | +15.219 | +113.815 | +540.720 |

However, the zero-threshold full path cover dropped to 78.335875 HOTA on
TrackEval. Pairwise utility is not additive when an internal source edge is
replaced: the local merge may be positive while the induced fragmentation of
the surrounding identity chain is strongly negative.

Freezing every source edge and allowing only parent-chain endpoint merges did
not repair the target mismatch: HOTA was 78.373140 and AssA 75.396520, despite
IDSW improving to 856. A two-block utility label also misprices a merge of two
whole parent tracks.

M23-12 changes the unit of action. One transaction splits the source track
after a proposed source chunk, splits the destination track before a proposed
destination chunk, then connects the source prefix to the destination suffix.
The label is the exact proxy contribution of all resulting chain segments
minus both original parent tracks. Restricting every parent track to at most
one transaction makes the selected action utilities additive.

A deterministic descending-utility greedy matching is only a lower bound on
the GT oracle, yet it reached 80.980587 HOTA / 80.289030 AssA. Per-sequence
HOTA was 81.240820 / 76.780814 / 81.638426 / 81.600970. This is a stricter and
more learnable action-space result than unrestricted chunk WIS.

## M23-12 strict LOSO learning result

The first signed-utility LOSO model retained useful sign AUC (0.754--0.901)
but ignored the asymmetric magnitude of false actions. High-ranked M03 and
M05 errors therefore had larger losses than the selected gains.

The second model explicitly estimated
`P(positive) * positive_gain - (1-P(positive)) * negative_loss`. Adding
GT-free prefix/suffix FastReID prototypes improved the top-10 net chain proxy
on three held sequences:

| Held sequence | sign AP | top-10 precision | top-10 net chain utility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MOT20-01 | 0.2623 | 0.70 | +450.741 |
| MOT20-02 | 0.2844 | 1.00 | +8052.737 |
| MOT20-03 | 0.1683 | 0.80 | +832.199 |
| MOT20-05 | 0.0985 | 0.00 | -21454.712 |

MOT20-05 is a hard sequence-domain failure, not a threshold-selection issue.
The semantic zero-expected-utility rule would select 4,150 M05 candidates
whose diagnostic total utility is strongly negative. No GT-free TrackEval
policy was executed from this model.

## Locked conclusions

1. The historical A42 long-gap action graph is closed: even its GT oracle is
   only 79.200060 HOTA.
2. The chunk-30 microtracklet graph is the first demonstrated action space
   with a greater-than-80 oracle on all four MOT20 training sequences.
3. Same-ID probability is the wrong optimization target; signed AssA utility
   ranks useful cross edges better.
4. Pairwise AssA utility is still insufficient for internal rewiring because
   it ignores chain-level opportunity cost.
5. The source-frozen endpoint control confirms that lower IDSW is not evidence
   of higher AssA or HOTA.
6. Disjoint chain transactions retain a 80.980587 HOTA GT-oracle lower bound;
   chain-level opportunity cost is therefore the active learning target.
7. Segment-level appearance resolves much of M01/M02/M03 ranking error, but
   does not transfer to M05.
8. The next safe model needs a training-only OOD/abstention rule or a
   domain-robust M05 representation, followed by nested sequence-LOSO. Until
   that succeeds, the fixed fused parent remains the valid reference and no
   M23-12 GT-free policy is deployment-eligible.
