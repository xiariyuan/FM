Candidate construction is GT-free and uses appearance discontinuity, motion error, rank/margin, consistency/mapping, side lengths, affected span, sequence percentiles, and diverse shortlist channels. Exact HOTA labels are computed only after the shortlist is frozen.

## Interpretation

M23-41 proved that cross-track deletion/replacement transactions alone were capped below 80. M23-43 crosses 80 by adding parent-track internal boundary cuts, so the combined action space is now sufficient in teacher-oracle capacity.

This result is not a deployable score and must not be submitted or mixed with strict OOF results. The next stage is a strict student built around deployable M23-39/M23-25 outer-training trackers. Training labels may use only the three outer-training sequences; held candidates must remain GT-free; policy selection must use inner exact TrackEval; the held tracker must be frozen before opening held GT.
