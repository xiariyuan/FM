## M23-34: domain-generalized ReID

M23-34 changes the upstream representation and regenerates the candidate graph.

### Model changes

1. Sequence-balanced P×K sampling with sequence quota proportional to the square root of training identity count.
2. A gradient-reversal sequence classifier to suppress camera/sequence-specific shortcuts.
3. Stronger color, grayscale and blur style augmentation for illumination and camera variation.
4. Per-training-sequence retrieval validation instead of pooled-only validation.
5. Robust checkpoint score: mean per-sequence mAP minus 0.5 times its standard deviation; worst-sequence mAP breaks ties.
6. The selected checkpoint re-embeds Phase-0 detections, rebuilds the micrograph, and then runs the frozen M23-25 transaction selector.

### Strict outer-M02 protocol

- Held sequence: MOT20-02.
- Training sequences: MOT20-01, MOT20-03 and MOT20-05 only.
- Eight epochs; batch size 64; four instances per identity.
- Sequence temperature 0.5; domain-adversarial weight 0.15; one warm-up epoch.
- Held MOT20-02 GT remains unavailable through training, re-embedding, graph generation and policy freezing.

Both the trainer and re-embedding smoke tests passed. The latter encoded 79/79 requested detections with zero failed images and reported held_gt_read=false.
