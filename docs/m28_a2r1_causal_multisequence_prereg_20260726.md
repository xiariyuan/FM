# M28-A2-R1 Strict Causal Multi-Sequence Repair

The original M28-A2 artifacts remain immutable. This repair regenerates candidate pools using only rows and ReID observations available at or before each unconfirmed decision frame. No full-sequence last-frame or future-overlap field may influence top-8 retrieval. GT is opened only after candidate freeze, first to screen dominant-identity-consistent actions and then to compute exact HOTA. Future identity conflicts are invalid teacher actions.

The repaired four-sequence gate retains the original requirements: every sequence positive HOTA, at least five positive valid actions per sequence, official combined delta at least +0.80 HOTA, and combined IDSW nonincrease. Student training remains prohibited until this gate passes.
